About Us

RAAZ Media Entertainment • Platform Overview

RAAZ Late Night Audio OTT is an independent digital audio drama and entertainment platform operated by Manish (Sole Proprietorship), headquartered in Gurugram, Haryana.

Our Mission & Artistic Vision

In an era of noisy screens and visual fatigue, RAAZ delivers cinematic, immersive audio storytelling designed specifically for late-night listening. We specialize in high-tension serialized Hindi audio fiction spanning romantic suspense, psychological intrigue, and emotional relationship dramas.

Original Productions & High-Fidelity Audio

Every story hosted on RAAZ is an original production featuring seasoned voice actors, ambient 3D soundscapes, and gripping cliffhangers. Content is delivered over a modern, lightweight web application accessible on all mobile devices and desktop browsers without requiring heavy app store downloads.

Commitment to Transparency & Compliance

  • Legal Self-Classification: Content is self-classified under Part III of the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 as Mature (18+ A) dramatic entertainment.
  • Statutory Compliance: We strictly adhere to Section 67 & 67A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Indecent Representation of Women (Prohibition) Act, 1986.
  • Consumer Autonomy: Clear upfront pricing with zero hidden traps, transparent NPCI/RBI AutoPay billing, and 1-click subscription management.
