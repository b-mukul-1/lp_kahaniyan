Privacy Policy & Discreet Billing

Last Updated: October 2026 • RAAZ Media Entertainment

RAAZ Media Entertainment (operated by Manish, Sole Proprietorship) is committed to safeguarding subscriber confidentiality and data privacy in strict compliance with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011.

1. Information We Collect

  • Mobile Number: Provided voluntarily during checkout or login to identify your active subscription pass.
  • Transaction Identifiers: Payment reference IDs, UMRN mandate identifiers, and subscription status returned by RBI-licensed payment aggregators (Razorpay / Cashfree / NPCI).
  • Technical Data: Browser user-agent and basic session cookies essential for maintaining playback progress.

2. Zero Financial Data Storage

All monetary transactions are processed through authorized payment aggregators operating under Reserve Bank of India regulations. We never collect, store, or process your UPI PIN, credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or internet banking passwords on our servers.

3. 100% Discreet Billing Guarantee

We recognize the private nature of late-night romantic drama entertainment. Your bank account statement, credit card bill, and UPI transaction history will display a neutral, generic merchant descriptor (such as RAAZ-DIGITAL or DIGITAL-SERVICES). No story titles, adult keywords, or sensitive labels are ever transmitted to banking or SMS payment receipts.

4. Data Protection & AES-256 Encryption

Mobile numbers and session tokens stored on our servers are encrypted using industry-standard 256-bit AES encryption. Access to subscriber records is restricted strictly to authorized support personnel for billing resolution.

5. Zero Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or distribute personal information or listening records to third-party marketing companies, advertisers, or data brokers.

6. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For any privacy or data inquiries, contact our Data Protection desk at manish@raazplay.online.
